Love Manifesto

Love in every country

A love poem, song or story from every country in the world, in the language it was written in and in English.

    About this project

    Love Manifesto gathers writing about love from every country of the world: poems, songs, ballads and stories, from medieval Persian verse to modern pop songs. Love is one of the things every culture writes about, and reading these side by side shows how differently, and how alike, people have put it into words.

    1. One for every country

      Each country has one chosen work. Some are famous, some are folk songs or oral traditions that are rarely heard outside their home.

    2. Read it in two languages

      Where a work is out of copyright, we show it in full, in its original language beside an English version.

    3. Follow the link

      Where it is still under copyright, we describe it and link to where you can read or listen to it.

    The book

    Manifesto for Love gathers 28 love poems from 28 countries across six continents.
