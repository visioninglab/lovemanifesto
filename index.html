    About this project

    Love Manifesto gathers writing about love from nearly every country in the world: poems, songs, ballads and stories, from medieval Persian verse to modern pop songs. Love is one of the things every culture writes about, and reading these side by side shows how differently, and how alike, people have put it into words.

    1. One for each country

      Each country has one chosen work. Some are famous, some are folk songs or oral traditions that are rarely heard outside their home.

    2. Read it in two languages

      Countries highlighted on the map have a poem you can read here, in its original language beside an English version.

    3. Explore the rest

      For the other countries, we describe the work and link to where you can read or listen to it.
